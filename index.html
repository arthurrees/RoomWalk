RoomWalk

1 · Start your plan

Two ways in:

  • Upload floor plan (top right) to trace over an image. It appears as a clean blueprint outline.
  • Start from scratch to draw on a blank 1-ft grid. Great when all you have is a 3D/listing render: keep it open for reference and rebuild the layout on the grid.

2 · Add your walls

Two ways. Try the automatic one first:

  • Detect walls (automatic): open Advanced ▾ and click Detect walls. It traces the walls straight off your plan in one click. Works best on plans drawn with solid black walls.
  • Draw them yourself: pick the Wall tool and click two points per wall (a start, then an end). Ends snap together and to straight horizontal/vertical lines. Perfect for touching up the auto-detect, or for plans it can't read.

Want to start over? Advanced ▾ → Clear walls. Your walls become the 3D room.

3 · Set the scale

Calibrate once so everything is life-size. Open Advanced ▾ and use any of:

  • Set scale from a dimension: click the two ends of a measurement printed on the plan (e.g. 24'-6"), then type it. Tip: use the longest dimension for best accuracy.
  • Or click a wall you've drawn and enter its real length.
  • Or Calibrate by area using the listing's square footage.

Switch feet/inches ↔ meters under Advanced → Units. The box (top-right) shows your footprint and floor area to double-check.

4 · Doors & windows

Two ways. Try the automatic one first:

  • Detect doors & windows (automatic): once your walls are in, open Advanced ▾ and click Detect doors & windows. It finds doorways and windows in the wall gaps. Works best on solid-wall plans; review and tidy up afterward.
  • Place them yourself: choose Door or Window, then click on a wall to drop it. Or, if you left an open gap where a doorway goes, hover over the gap and it auto-fits a door/window to fill it.
  • Adjust: with the Pan cursor, click one to select, then drag the middle to move it, drag an end to resize, or press Delete to remove.

5 · Furniture & fixtures

Click Items, choose a piece, then click on the plan to drop it. It auto-fits tight gaps and is selected right away.

  • Drag to move; drag the red handles or use the panel to resize. Items stop at walls instead of passing through.
  • R rotates · Delete removes.
  • A red ⚠ means it overlaps a wall or another item, so nudge it clear.
  • Wall-mounted items (the Wall group: cabinets, shelves, TV, mirror) snap onto a wall at a height. Drag to slide them along the wall; use Height −/+ in the panel to raise or lower.

6 · Style it

  • Whole home: under Advanced ▾ → Style, pick a floor material (wood / tile / carpet / concrete) and a wall colour.
  • One wall: click a wall (in 2D or 3D) and set just its colour in the panel.
  • One room: in 3D, click a room's floor and choose its material, so rooms can differ (tile bathroom, wood living room).

7 · Deleting things

With the Pan cursor, click any wall, door, window, or furniture piece to select it, then press Delete (or Backspace) to remove it. Works in 2D, 3D, and Walk.

  • Deleting a door or window placed across a gap reopens the gap, so it won't leave a solid wall behind.
  • To clear a whole category at once, use Advanced ▾ → Clear walls / Clear doors/windows / Clear furniture.

8 · View in 3D & Walk

  • 3D: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. You can place & move items here too.
  • Walk: click-drag to look, WASD / arrows to move, Q/E to turn. You're 5'9" tall and walls/furniture are solid.

9 · Save & undo

Your work autosaves in this browser and reloads automatically next time. Made a mistake? Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo (or the ↶ ↷ buttons).

Save downloads your whole project (plan + walls + everything) as one file; Open loads it back later or on another computer.
