Furnish and style

Make the plan feel like home

Select Items, choose a piece, and click anywhere in 2D, 3D, or Walk to place it.

Resize: drag item handles or use the selection panel. Press R to rotate.

Style: select a wall to recolour it, or a room in 3D to change its floor.

Reach behind walls: select an object and choose Hide. Use “Show hidden objects” in the right panel to restore everything.

Delete: select anything and press Delete, or use the selection panel.
